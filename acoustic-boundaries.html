Compare small-amplitude pressure waves in an ideal one-dimensional tube with independently selectable end conditions.

Linear acoustics: this numerical demonstration models weak pressure disturbances about a uniform equilibrium. It does not calculate finite-amplitude shock interactions or flow outside the tube.

Input Parameters

Closed end: zero velocity perturbation. Open end: zero pressure perturbation (equilibrium ambient pressure).

All axes and perturbations are normalized; no physical pressure or tube dimensions are required.
